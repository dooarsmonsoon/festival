Travel Information

How to Reach

Your journey to the Dooars Monsoon Music & Cultural Festival 3.0 at 21 Basti, Rajabhatkhawa

Festival Location

21 Basti is a small and peaceful forest village located in Rajabhatkhawa, within the Buxa Tiger Reserve, in the Alipurduar district of West Bengal.

PIN: 735227

By Train

Visitors travelling by train can reach either of the following nearby railway stations:

  • Alipurduar Junction (APDJ)
  • New Alipurduar (NOQ)

From the railway station, you can continue your journey towards Rajabhatkhawa and 21 Basti by car, taxi, shared taxi, auto-rickshaw or other locally available transport.

From Railway Station to 21 Basti

After reaching Alipurduar Junction or New Alipurduar, visitors can choose from several local transport options to reach the festival venue.

Private Car

You can book a private car or local cab directly to 21 Basti.

Shared Taxi

Shared taxis are a convenient option for budget travellers.

Auto-Rickshaw

Local auto-rickshaws may also be available for the onward journey.

Local Transport

Other locally available vehicles can also be used depending on availability.

By Road

21 Basti is accessible by road through Rajabhatkhawa. Visitors travelling by private vehicle or hired car can travel towards Rajabhatkhawa and then continue towards 21 Basti.

As the festival is located in a forest area, visitors are advised to plan their journey in advance and allow sufficient travel time.

Local Transport

Local transportation options may include:

  • Private car / hired car
  • Local taxi
  • Shared taxi
  • Auto-rickshaw
  • Other locally available transport

Important Travel Information

  • The festival venue is located in a forest village, so transportation options may be limited at certain times.
  • It is recommended to arrange your return transportation in advance.
  • During the monsoon season, allow additional travel time due to weather and road conditions.
  • Carry rain protection such as an umbrella or raincoat.
  • Please respect the local community and the natural environment of the Buxa Tiger Reserve.
