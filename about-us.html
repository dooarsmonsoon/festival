About the Festival

Dooars Monsoon Music & Cultural Festival 3.0

Dooars Monsoon Music & Cultural Festival 3.0 is a three-day celebration of nature, music, culture and community, set against the beautiful monsoon landscape of Dooars.

The festival brings local tribal and rural communities to the heart of the celebration, giving them a platform to showcase and sell their traditional food, handicrafts, handmade products and local produce through small community stalls.

A key purpose of the festival is to support rural livelihoods, promote local culture and create meaningful economic opportunities for the people of Dooars, while respecting and celebrating the natural environment.

Across three days, visitors can enjoy music, folk and cultural performances, local cuisine, community activities and the unique monsoon atmosphere of Dooars.
